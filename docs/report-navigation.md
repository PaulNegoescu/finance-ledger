# Report filters and spending groups

Overview, Spending and Comparison store applied filters in their URLs. Reload or use
browser Back/Forward to restore the account and period. Spending also preserves its
currency and group/category view; Overview preserves the selected chart year.
Draft form edits change the URL only when applied. Reapplying the same filters refreshes
the report. Invalid incoming dates, account IDs or display options reset to safe defaults
with a visible notice.

Spending defaults to group totals, with a selector for the category chart. Group totals
sum the current categories within the selected currency and use exact integer amounts.
Ungrouped includes categories without a group and uncategorized transactions. The full
group breakdown remains available below the chart; category rows also show their group
and retain transaction drill-down links. Groups classify recorded money out; transfers
remain included and savings balances are not inferred.
